Control your Bluetooth LED strips right from your iPhone – fast, beautiful and without detours.

Lichtpilot connects to your lights by itself. On the home screen every light glows in its current colour – tap one to control just that light, or control all of them together.

PICK A COLOUR WITH THE CAMERA
• Point the camera at anything – a cushion, a poster, a drink – and your light glows live in exactly that colour

LIGHT
• On/off and a big brightness bar, for each light or all at once
• Colour wheel, white tone bar and your recently used colours
• Save scenes and recall them with a single tap
• Good night: all lights turn warm and fade out over 10 minutes
• Wake up: a 90-second sunrise

EFFECTS
• Eight smooth effects: Rainbow, Aurora, Sunset, Ocean, Lava, Neon, Breathing and Candlelight – softly blended, no jumps
• Wave: with several lights, the colour travels from light to light
• Music mode: the lights pulse to the beat of your music. The app only measures the volume, nothing is recorded.
• Adjustable speed
• Plus the built-in effects of your lights

LIGHTS
• Add new lights with one tap – only compatible LED lights are shown
• Your own names like "Living room" or "Bed"
• Blink, rename, connect and disconnect
• Reconnects by itself after dropouts

CONTROL CENTRE, LOCK SCREEN AND SIRI
• Buttons for Control Centre and the Lock Screen: on/off and good night
• Home Screen widgets
• Scenes and brightness in the Shortcuts app – for automations and the Action button

COMPATIBLE LIGHTS
Bluetooth LED strips and lamps that show up as "ELK-BLEDOM" or "MELK". These controllers are built into many affordable LED strips. Without a light, you can try the app in demo mode.

The app is in German.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. The app collects no data and needs no internet connection.
